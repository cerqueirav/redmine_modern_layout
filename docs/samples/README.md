# Sample images

Images to try Redmine Modern Layout in *Administration > Appearance* and to take screenshots.
All organizations shown in them are fictional, except Redmine itself.

| File | Upload it in | This sample | Ideal for your own image |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sample-logo.png` | Identity > Logo | 1750×312 (5.6:1), transparent | Horizontal **4:1**, e.g. 1200×300 px, transparent PNG or SVG. Shown 46 px tall on the menu (max. 190 px wide) and up to 120 px on the login page |
| `sample-icon.png` | Identity > Browser tab icon (also the collapsed side menu) | 256×256 (1:1), transparent | Square **1:1**, 256×256 px, symbol only, transparent |
| `sample-partners.png` | Login page > Partner banner | 1496×120 (12.5:1), transparent | Strip **12:1**, e.g. 1400×116 px, dark logos on transparent background (shown on white, up to 56 px tall) |
| `sample-login-background.jpg` | Login page > Background image | 1920×1280 (3:2) | Landscape **3:2**, 1920×1280 px (min. 1600 px wide), JPG or WEBP. Cropped to fill the area: keep the subject in the center |

`sample-partners.png` and `sample-login-background.jpg` are generated by `generate_samples.py`
(Pillow + Ubuntu Sans font). "Acme Health", "Bluepeak", "Riverstone" and "Nova Lab" are fictional.
